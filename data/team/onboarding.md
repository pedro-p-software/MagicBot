# New-member onboarding

Updated: replace this line whenever onboarding changes.

Welcome to Magic Island Robotics! This checklist needs to be completed by a team lead with the current onboarding process:

1. Add the official communication channel and calendar link.
2. State the first meeting date, time, and location.
3. Link the safety and lab rules.
4. Explain how members choose or join a subteam.
5. Name the approved person/channel for questions.
